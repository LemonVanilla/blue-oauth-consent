Drive Tools: Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-18

Drive Tools is a private tool used by one person, its owner, on their own laptop. It runs rclone to tidy app build files in the owner's own Google Drive, keeping the newest builds and deleting older ones. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it can access

  • The owner's own Google Drive, so it can list app build files and delete old ones.
  • The Google account's email address, to confirm which account was connected.

What it keeps

  • The access token Google issues, stored only on the owner's own laptop and used only for this cleanup.
  • A short log of which build files it deleted, on the same laptop.

What it doesn't do

Nothing is copied off the Drive, shared, sold, or used for any other purpose, and no one else's data is involved. No analytics, ads or tracking.

Removing access

Google Account → Security → Third-party access → rclone → Remove access. Cleanup stops immediately; files already in Drive stay as they are.
