Roofbook: Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-09

Roofbook is a private home records app for one household's own use. It is not offered to the public.

What Roofbook keeps

  • Your records: your homes and their details (such as the address, year built and purchase date, if you enter them), the systems and items in them, the History entries with their costs, companies, warranties and notes, the maintenance schedules, the projects, your settings and the optional profile you fill in (display name, gender, birth month and year). They are stored on your phone. There is no account until you choose a plan that needs one, and without Sync they stay only on your phone.
  • Maintenance reminders are set on the phone itself and need no account.

Google sign-in and your Drive (optional)

  • You don't have to sign in. Roofbook works fully on your phone without an account. Roofbook asks you to sign in with Google only when you choose a plan that needs it (Daily Backup or Sync).
  • The Google account's email address and user ID, used only to identify the account that owns the plan.
  • Your plan: a record of which Roofbook plan the account has, kept in Roofbook's database with the account's user ID (and, for a plan set before you first sign in, the email it was set for). The plan is set by hand; nothing is bought in the app.
  • With Daily Backup: access to the files Roofbook itself makes in your Google Drive, and nothing else there. Roofbook cannot see your other Drive files, Gmail or contacts.
  • A backup is a copy of your records and, if you choose, your Preferences (reminder settings, theme and vibration strength), saved to a Roofbook Backup folder in your own Google Drive. That copy is yours, and you can delete it there at any time. It never includes your AI keys, your profile or your sign-in.

Sync (optional)

  • You don't have to use it. Without Sync, your records stay only on your phone.
  • With Sync, your records (the homes, systems, items, History entries, maintenance schedules and projects, and the order you keep them in) are kept on your account on Roofbook's server, so they reach your other phones and anyone you invite.
  • Your display name is kept with your account. If you share a home, the people you invite into it can see its records, each other's display names and who changed what.
  • When Sync ends, the copy on your account is kept through the end of the next full calendar month, then deleted on the 1st after it. Your records stay on your phone.

AI helpers (optional)

  • You don't have to use them. If you save your own key from Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in the app, the receipt and warranty scan sends the photo you take or pick, made smaller first, straight from your phone to that company, which reads it and sends back the text. It never comes to Roofbook.
  • Roofbook keeps only the text it fills in, and only when you save the entry. The photo is never kept. What the AI company does with it is up to its own privacy terms, and it bills your key, not Roofbook.
  • Your key stays on this phone in its secure storage and is never backed up, exported or sent to Roofbook.

Blue Connector (optional)

  • You don't have to use it. It comes with Sync: you add it to your own AI app (such as Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini) and sign in there with the Google account you use in Roofbook.
  • Your AI app can then read the homes you're in as Sync keeps them on Roofbook's server, and change them only after you say yes to each change in your AI app. Roofbook never sees your chat.
  • For each read or change, Roofbook keeps what was done, the record's name, when, and which AI app it came from, for 90 days, so you can see it in Settings → Blue Connector. A request that didn't come from a known AI app also shows a rough place (city, region, country) worked out from its internet address, with IP geolocation by DB-IP.
  • Signed in, Roofbook keeps your phone's time zone (such as America/Chicago) with your account, so dates are your own day.
  • Sign out all AI apps signs every AI app out at once. Wipe all data and Delete account delete this activity.

Feedback (optional)

  • Feedback you send from the app: your message and the kind of feedback you picked, the Roofbook version, your plan, the phone's make, model and Android version, the gender you chose in your profile and your age range (never your birth date), a reply email only if you give one and, if signed in, the account email and user ID. It is stored in Roofbook's database and emailed to Roofbook's feedback inbox.
  • When you are not signed in, a salted, one-way code made from the phone's internet address is kept only to limit how much feedback can be sent.

Diagnostics (optional)

  • Roofbook keeps a technical log on your phone of what the app did over the last 48 hours: which buttons were tapped (by their internal name), which screens opened, and any errors Roofbook hit. It never includes your records, your profile, your email, your keys or anything you typed.
  • It stays on your phone until you choose to send it, with feedback or from Settings. Then the last hour of it is sent, with the Roofbook version and the phone's make, model and Android version, tied to your account when signed in, or otherwise to a random ID made for this install. Each report is kept so problems can be found over time, until Wipe all data or Delete account deletes it.

Who else is involved

  • Google, for sign-in and for the backups in your own Drive.
  • The AI company you pick, only for the scans you start, on your own key.
  • The AI app you connect to Blue Connector, only for what you ask it to do.
  • DB-IP, whose free IP geolocation data gives the rough place for a request not from a known AI app.
  • If paid plans are offered, purchases are handled by Google Play. Roofbook never sees card details.

What it doesn't do

Nothing is sold or used for advertising. There are no ads, analytics or crash-reporting tools, and no location tracking.

Your choices

  • Everything can be exported or deleted in the app, under Settings → Data. Wipe all data clears the phone and, when signed in, the diagnostics reports and Blue Connector activity tied to your account and your records on Roofbook's server (a home only you own is deleted, and you leave a home shared with other owners), and keeps your account and plan. Delete account also removes your plan record, your profile and time zone, the feedback and diagnostics you sent, and your Google sign-in once no other app still uses it. Your Drive backups stay in your Drive. You can also ask for deletion on the account and data deletion page.
  • To remove Roofbook's access to your Google account: Google Account → Security → Third-party access → Roofbook → Remove access.
