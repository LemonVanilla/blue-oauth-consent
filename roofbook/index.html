Roofbook

Home records for Android

Roofbook is a home records app made by Blue Hour. For each home it keeps the systems, the items in them and every install, replacement, repair, maintenance visit and renewal done to them, with costs, companies and warranties. It plans projects and reminds you about recurring maintenance. This copy of Roofbook is for one household's own use and is not listed in any app store.

Roofbook works fully on your phone without an account. Signing in with Google is optional: it is used only to identify the account that owns the plan, and Google Drive only for the backups Roofbook makes itself.
