Roofbook: Terms of Use

Last updated 2026-11-08

Roofbook is a private home records app for one household's own use. It is not offered to the public.

Your records

Roofbook keeps what you enter about your home. Totals, warranty dates and the warranties-ending count come from those entries, so they are only as complete as what you put in. Keep your original receipts, warranty cards and contracts: Roofbook is a record of them, not a replacement.

Reminders

Maintenance reminders are a convenience. Android may deliver one late or not at all, for example in battery saving, so don't rely on them for anything safety-critical such as smoke alarms or gas appliances.

AI scans

The receipt and warranty scan is optional and uses your own key with the AI company you pick. It can misread a paper, so check every field it fills in before you save. Its use is also covered by that company's own terms.

Plans

Free keeps everything on your phone. Daily Backup copies your records to your own Google Drive. Sync keeps them on your account so they reach your other phones and the people you share a home with. Your plan is set on your account; nothing is sold in the app.

Your data

Your records stay yours. How they are handled is described in the privacy policy.

No warranty

Roofbook is provided as is, without any warranty. It is not professional, legal, insurance or home-inspection advice.

Age

Roofbook is intended for people 13 and older.
